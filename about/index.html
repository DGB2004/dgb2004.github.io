Fix the right thing first.

You probably already know three or four things that slow your week down. The hard part is knowing which one to fix first, and finding the time to work it out.

When AI tools got good, I tested them on my own work first. The lesson was simple: the tool matters far less than picking the right job and cutting the wasted steps before anything gets automated.

So that's how this works. You show me one job, we clear out what shouldn't be there, and I set up whatever fits your business, not a one-size-fits-all tool. Then the next one, only if it's worth it to you.

The point is your week back, not more software.
